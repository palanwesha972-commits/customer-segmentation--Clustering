Customer Segmentation Using Python and Clustering

1. Project Overview

This project focuses on customer segmentation using Python and machine learning techniques. The goal is to analyze customer data, identify different customer groups, and understand their purchasing behavior.

2. Project Objective

- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
- Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
- Explore patterns and trends using data visualization.
- Segment customers into different groups using clustering.
- Develop customer profiles to support targeted marketing strategies.
- Help businesses improve customer engagement and marketing decisions.

3. Dataset Description

The project uses a marketing campaign dataset containing customer information, income, purchase history, campaign responses, and other demographic details.

Some important features include:

- "ID" – Customer identification number.
- "Year_Birth" – Customer's birth year.
- "Education" – Customer's education level.
- "Marital_Status" – Customer's marital status.
- "Income" – Customer's income.
- "Dt_Customer" – Date when the customer enrolled.
- "Recency" – Days since the customer's last purchase.
- "MntWines", "MntFruits", "MntMeatProducts" – Spending on different product categories.
- "NumWebPurchases" – Number of purchases made through the website.
- "NumStorePurchases" – Number of purchases made in stores.
- "Response" – Response to a marketing campaign.

5. Project Workflow

Step 1: Data Loading

Loaded the marketing campaign dataset into Python using Pandas.

Step 2: Data Exploration

Used "head()", "shape", "info()", "describe()", and "isnull().sum()" to understand the dataset, its structure, and missing values.

Step 3: Data Cleaning and Preprocessing

Checked missing values, examined data types, converted the customer enrollment date into a datetime format, and prepared relevant features for analysis.

Step 4: Feature Engineering

Created additional features such as customer age and total spending to better understand customer behavior.

Step 5: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Used visualizations to explore income distribution, campaign responses, spending patterns, and customer purchasing behavior.

Step 6: Clustering

Applied a clustering approach to group customers with similar characteristics and purchasing patterns.

Step 7: Customer Profiling

Analyzed the characteristics of the customer groups to understand differences in spending, income, and purchasing behavior.

6. Key Analysis

- Customer income distribution.
- Customer age and spending patterns.
- Product-category spending.
- Marketing campaign responses.
- Similarities and differences among customer groups.

7. Business Benefits

- Supports targeted marketing campaigns.
- Helps identify high-value customer groups.

- Supports data-driven marketing decisions.
- Helps businesses plan more personalized customer engagement.

8. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python, data analysis, visualization, and clustering can be used to understand customer behavior and identify meaningful customer segments. The findings can help businesses develop more effective and targeted marketing strategies.
